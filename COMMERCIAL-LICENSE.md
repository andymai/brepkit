# Commercial Licensing

brepkit is dual-licensed. The open-source license, [AGPL-3.0-only](./LICENSE), requires that
applications and network services built on brepkit make their own source available under the
AGPL. A commercial license from Collective Context, LLC removes that requirement.

## When you need a commercial license

- Embedding brepkit (including `brepkit-wasm` or `@brepkit/wasm`) in a proprietary application
- Offering a product or hosted service built on brepkit without releasing its source under the AGPL
- Redistributing brepkit as part of a closed-source SDK, plugin, or toolkit

## When the AGPL is enough

- Open-source projects that comply with the AGPL
- Research and personal projects
- Internal tools and evaluation
